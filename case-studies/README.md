# Case Studies

Case studies are learning artifacts. They are not product truth.

## Types

* Version snapshots: short records of what the product version looked like when read.
* Product evolution case studies: focused explanations of how a product area changed across versions.
* Architecture case studies: deeper studies of product design, boundaries, and runtime flows.
* Workflow case studies: studies of engineering process, validation loops, context use, and handoff quality.

Always point back to product repo files instead of duplicating product source documents.
